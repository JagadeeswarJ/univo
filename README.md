# 🎓 Student & Organizer Event Portal

## 🚀 What’s It All About?

The **Student & Organizer Event Portal** is your go-to hub for everything event-related on campus. Whether you're a student looking for fun and enriching events or an organizer trying to get the word out, this platform brings everyone together in one simple, powerful space.

Think of it as a digital bridge connecting students and event organizers to make event planning, participation, and discovery easier than ever.

Our goals:  
✔️ Boost student engagement  
✔️ Help everyone build skills  
✔️ Encourage meaningful networking  
✔️ Promote events campus-wide  

---

## 🌟 What You Can Do

### 🧭 Find Events That Match *You*
- Scroll through upcoming, live, and past events
- Filter events by:
  - **Type** (Tech, Cultural, Sports—you name it)
  - **Location**
  - **Date**
  - **Tags** based on your interests

### 📅 Stay on Top of Things with a Visual Calendar
- See all campus events at a glance
- Color codes make it easy to spot what’s coming up, what’s happening now, and what you’ve missed

### 👤 Personalized Dashboard for Students
- See what you’ve signed up for
- Get timely reminders so you don’t miss out
- Track your event history
- Collect badges, earn points, and climb the leaderboard

### 🛠️ Simple Tools for Organizers
- Create and manage your events in minutes
- Add ticket options (free or paid)
- Upload event posters and details
- Set participant caps
- Manage sign-ups and waitlists effortlessly

### 📢 Get Real-Time Updates
- Students get notified instantly about:
  - Time or venue changes
  - Event cancellations
  - Announcements and important info

### 🏆 A Bit of Fun with Gamification
- Earn cool badges and points for being active
- Check the leaderboard and see how you rank among your peers

## 🔐 Safe & Secure Access

- Role-based access—students and organizers see what’s relevant to them
- Powered by **Firebase Auth** for secure login and session management
